Collect & clean

A clean collection before you analyze

Search six sources at once, or import Scopus, Web of Science, PubMed, and RIS.

Merge duplicates across sources, unify keyword synonyms and author name spellings, fill in reference lists through Crossref, and add journal quartiles from SJR.

  • Cross-source duplicate merging
  • Keyword synonyms apply to every analysis
  • Metadata quality report per field
Knowledge structure

See the shape of a field

Thematic map, theme evolution, co-occurrence networks, co-citation, bibliographic coupling, historiograph, and RPYS.

Full descriptive analysis: annual production, citations, Bradford's and Lotka's laws, countries and international collaboration. Network charts can be zoomed, panned, and saved as SVG or PNG.

Gap finding

Gap candidates, not guesses

Opportunity themes and Gap Explorer combine several detectors into one ranked list.

Each candidate has novelty, evidence, and momentum scores, the documents that support it, and suggested research questions. Test your own gap ideas, then verify them against recent literature outside your collection before you write.

  • Novelty · evidence · momentum scores
  • Review: accepted, needs check, rejected
  • Re-check against papers from the last two years
Output

From gap to chapter draft

A research gap draft plus a methods & results draft, ready to edit.

Choose APA 7 or IEEE citations, in Indonesian or English. Export BibTeX, RIS, CSV, and chart images for your manuscript.

Frequently asked questions

Is Celah free?

Yes. Celah is free for everyone and open-source under the MIT license. There is no account, subscription, or project limit.

What is a research gap, and how does Celah find one?

A research gap is a topic, or a link between topics, that has not been studied much. Celah maps a field's literature bibliometrically, then runs several detectors (such as keyword pairs that rarely appear together, emerging themes that are not yet connected, and future work the authors mention) to build a list of gap candidates with scores and evidence documents.

What data can I use?

You can search OpenAlex, Crossref, Semantic Scholar, PubMed, Europe PMC, and arXiv directly, or import exports from Scopus (CSV, BibTeX, RIS), Web of Science, PubMed, and RIS files from Zotero, Mendeley, or EndNote.

Is my research data uploaded to a server?

No. All collections and analysis results stay on your computer. Celah only contacts the research data sources you choose, over HTTPS, and sends no telemetry.

How is it different from VOSviewer or Bibliometrix?

VOSviewer focuses on network visualization, and Bibliometrix is an R package (with the biblioshiny interface) for bibliometric analysis. Celah covers bibliometric analysis and knowledge maps, then carries on to research gap candidates with evidence documents, checks against recent literature, and a chapter draft, with no R install or coding.

Can I use the results directly in my thesis?

Celah drafts a research gap section and a methods and results section with APA 7 or IEEE citations, in Indonesian or English. Treat the drafts as a starting point: check the evidence documents, numbers, and wording before using them in your writing.

Which operating systems are supported?

macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel), Windows 10/11 64-bit, and Linux (AppImage, .deb, .rpm). Celah updates itself from Settings › About › Check for updates.

Do I need an internet connection?

You need internet to search for documents, fill in references, and check gaps against recent literature. Analyzing a collection you already have runs entirely on your computer.
